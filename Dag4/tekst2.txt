Online influencers claim you need to learn dozens of technologies to become a good .NET developer.
This creates unnecessary anxiety and distracts from what actually matters. Nemanja argues you should focus on mastering
.NET fundamentals instead of chasing every new tool. The key areas include understanding .NET internals, modern C# 
features, performance optimization, and core libraries. Learning specialized technologies like Redis or GraphQL is 
only needed when your job requires them.
